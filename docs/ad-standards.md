# Channel Z0 — Ad Standards (Station Bulletin № 1)

*The written standard the build guide tells you to have. Post it, link it from
the site, point to it when declining a spot. One page, on purpose.*

---

## Who can advertise

Anyone local: your shop, your band, your garage sale, your league, your
inexplicable hobby. Airtime is free while the station is small. Businesses
outside the neighbourhood get a polite no — this channel's whole premise is
that it's from here.

## Technical requirements

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Length | 15, 30, or 60 seconds — exactly |
| Container | MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio) |
| Picture | 1080p or 720p, landscape |
| Audio | Anything reasonable — the station loudness-normalizes every spot |

Spots that arrive close-but-not-exact get trimmed or padded to standard at the
station's discretion (`tools/normalize-ad.sh` handles the rest).

## Content rules

1. **You own everything in it.** Footage, images, and especially music. A
   submission is an attestation that you hold the rights. No exceptions —
   rights problems put the whole station at risk, not just your spot.
2. **Neighbourly.** A kid might be watching; Z0 plays all day in people's
   houses. Nothing you wouldn't say across a fence.
3. **No politics, no feuds.** Campaigns, causes, and grievances with your
   neighbour's hedge all go somewhere else.
4. **No scams, no miracle products, nothing illegal.** Obviously — but it's
   written down now.
5. **Weirdness is welcome.** Strange is not a violation. Boring is closer to
   one.

## Process

Email your spot (or a link to it) using the button on the site. Every
submission is watched by a human before air. Accepted spots are normalized
and enter the ad-break rotation — no fixed air dates while we're small, but
breaks run all day, every day.

The station reserves the right to decline or retire any spot at any time,
with love, and without debate. This is a living document; the current version
in the repo is the one that applies.
