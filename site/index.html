FORMATLINEAR TV · 854×480 One channel, one stream, no menu — you get what's on. 854×480, 16:9: standard definition on purpose, because nearly every print in the library is a 480p scan. The programme itself is 4:3, pillarboxed into the middle 640×480, and the two 107-pixel bars either side are where the listings, the date and the weather live — so the station furniture never sits on the picture.
ENCODEH.264 · 1600k · AAC H.264 at 1600 kbps (3200 kbps buffer) with AAC audio, encoded once on the station's own GPU. Hardware NVENC, so running a channel around the clock costs the house machine almost nothing.
PLAYOUTERSATZTV 26.7 · YAML The week is a file, not a click-path. ErsatzTV reads one YAML schedule and builds the broadcast day from it — every block, tentpole and sign-off in the grid below comes out of that single document.
GRAPHICSBUG · CARD · STRIP · NEXT Four things painted over the picture as it goes out: the channel bug, a weather card that refreshes every 15 minutes, the bottom strip, and the NEXT lower third naming what follows — it shows in a programme's first 30 seconds, then gets out of the way.
WEATHERKELOWNA · OPEN-METEO Local forecast from Open-Meteo — no API key, no account, nobody counting you. It drives the corner card every 15 minutes and a full-screen WEATHER DESK segment four times a day.
TOWEROWNCAST · PASSTHROUGH The transmitter: one small always-on VM running Owncast in single-quality passthrough. It re-muxes and never re-encodes, which is the only reason a machine that size can carry a 24/7 channel.
UPTIME24/7 · SIGN-OFF 00:00 The transmitter never sleeps; the broadcast day does. Sign-on at 06:00, sign-off at midnight, test card until sunrise — and Saturday runs late, as Saturday should.
ADSLOCALLY SOURCED Every commercial comes from someone nearby — a shop, a band, a garage sale. Screened before air and loudness-normalized, so no spot is louder than the film it interrupts.
SPONSORRIPOSTE LABS Riposte Laboratories keeps the transmitter lit and fills the gaps between programmes with whatever the lab has been building that week. No other advertiser pays for this channel.
STATUSSTANDBY The station's vitals, polled from the tower every 30 seconds. SIGNAL ACQUIRED means the uplink is connected and segments are genuinely moving — not merely that this page loaded.
P2PSTANDBY Viewers quietly hand each other stream segments over WebRTC, so the tower ships each one far fewer times. Strictly a bonus: no WebRTC, or no one else watching, and it's ordinary HLS with nothing lost.

SEC.05 Rights Desk PUBLIC DOMAIN ONLY

Half of this channel is made here: Ground Zero, Lab Hour, the Neighbourhood Desk, the sign-on and the sign-off, the idents and the intervals between programmes. The other half was fetched from the Internet Archive, and every second of it carried a public-domain licence mark before it was downloaded.

The intake tool never asks for anything else — the search itself is filtered to public-domain-marked items, so material without a mark is never even a candidate. For every file that lands, the station records the licence URL it matched and the archive.org identifier it came from, and names the file after that identifier. Anything you see on air can be traced back to the item it came from and the mark it was aired under.

A licence mark is the uploader's claim, not a legal opinion. It is the best signal the archive offers and it is the one this station acts on. If you hold rights in something we are airing, use the desk — the programme comes off the schedule while we check, not after.
